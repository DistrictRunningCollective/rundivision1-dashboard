The ecosystem

One platform. Three expressions.

RunDivision connects the year-round infrastructure crews need with a visible championship property and a media platform that tells the stories behind the movement.

01 / The Foundation

Member Association

A formal affiliation for established community-led running organizations. Members gain shared infrastructure, collective opportunities, funding pathways, industry relationships, and a voice in shaping the network.

02 / The Stage

RunDivision Championships

A national crew-first competition where member organizations represent their cities, compete as teams, earn points, and pursue the Performance Championship, Club Cup, and Impact Cup.

03 / The Story

RunDivision Studios

Original storytelling around crews, athletes, cities, and community impact. The media platform extends RunDivision beyond race day and creates year-round value for members, partners, and audiences.

Why affiliate?

Membership should create leverage.

RunDivision is not asking crews to give up what they built. The association exists to make things that are difficult alone more accessible together.

01 · Funding

Paid Partnerships

Access to national and multi-city brand opportunities negotiated through the collective scale of the network.

02 · Capital

Member Grants

Funding pathways for local programming, events, youth initiatives, travel, community impact, and organizational capacity.

03 · Competition

Championship Access

Only affiliated member organizations can compete for official RunDivision cup titles, season points, and the overall championship.

04 · Infrastructure

Organizational Support

Shared standards, operating tools, partnership guidance, safety resources, vendor information, and leadership development.

06 · Influence

Collective Voice

A seat in a larger conversation about how community-led running is funded, represented, supported, and developed.

05 · Media

Amplification

National visibility through RunDivision Studios, crew profiles, championship coverage, and original storytelling.

Member affiliation

How membership works.

Crews join RunDivision as independent member organizations. Membership creates the year-round relationship; the Championships are one of its most visible benefits.

01

Apply as an organization

Established crews provide organizational information, leadership contacts, programming details, and alignment with shared standards.

02

Become an affiliated member

The organization completes its annual membership agreement and financial or operational buy-in.

03

Establish a championship roster

Member crews submit one seasonal roster. Runners confirm a single RunDivision crew affiliation for competition.

04

Activate the network

Members participate in championships, partnerships, grants, leadership conversations, media, and shared initiatives.

05

Grow together

Stronger organizations increase the reach, influence, and economic power of the entire RunDivision ecosystem.
