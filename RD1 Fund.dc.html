The concept

A fund connected to participation.

The Community Fund is part of the RD1 Season Zero launch. Crews enter the network, build a verified profile, participate in Season Zero, and become eligible to apply for funding that advances running in their city.

Season Zero process

Enter. Participate. Apply. Build.

The fund rewards meaningful participation in the RD1 ecosystem, not one-time entry for a chance to win.

Phase 01
Founding Entry

Crews submit their profile, city, leadership contact, reach, and community work.

Phase 02
Season Zero

Crews participate through races, community activity, storytelling, or network programming.

Phase 03
Grant Application

Eligible crews submit a focused project plan, budget, timeline, and intended outcomes.

Phase 04
Award + Report

Selected projects receive funding and share implementation updates through the dashboard.

Crew entry

Start with your crew profile.

This short interest form collects initial demand. The full application routes to a dedicated crew entry form.

Proposed eligibility
  • — Complete a verified RD1 crew profile
  • — Participate in Season Zero programming or competition
  • — Share one measurable example of community impact
  • — Submit a realistic project plan and budget
  • — Agree to document the use and impact of awarded funds
  • — Remain in good standing through the grant period
