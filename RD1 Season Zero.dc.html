Why the routes matter
The crews built the culture. Now they map the sport.

RD1 circuits are not generic virtual race courses. Each route is selected by the local crew based on the places, streets and neighborhoods that define how their community experiences running.

Season Zero turns those routes into league infrastructure. A digital challenge today can become an official RD1 gathering, a city event, or a championship course tomorrow.

Digital circuit → City Day → Physical event → RD1 Championship
