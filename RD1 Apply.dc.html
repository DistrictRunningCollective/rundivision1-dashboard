Membership application

Bring your crew into RunDivision.

RunDivision is open to independent, community-led run crews of any size or city. We're not looking for a certain look or a certain size — we're looking for organizations that already show up for their runners, week after week.

Every application is reviewed by a person, not a filter. That takes a little longer, but it keeps the network safe and the standard real.

What we look for

Open door, real standards.

We welcome

  • — Crews of any size, pace range, or founding date
  • — Any city, neighborhood, or region — we're not limited to the ten founding markets
  • — Run-walk groups, training clubs, and identity- or community-based crews alike
  • — Volunteer-run organizations without formal nonprofit or LLC status

We require

  • — At least 6 months of regular, public group runs
  • — A named, reachable point of contact who is 18 or older
  • — A written commitment to a safe, non-discriminatory space for all runners
  • — Agreement to RD1's code of conduct and event safety guidelines
  • — No pending safety complaints, bans, or unresolved incidents with another run organization
After you submit

What happens next.

Step 1
Application review

A member of the RD1 team reads every application. Typical turnaround is 5–7 business days.

Step 2
A short call

We talk through your crew's programming, safety practices, and what membership would mean for you.

Step 3
Decision

Approved, or we'll tell you directly what's missing and invite you to reapply once it's in place.

Step 4
Onboarding

Sign the membership agreement, submit your roster, and get access to the network.

Before you start

Have this ready.

  • — Your crew's founding year and home city
  • — A rough active member count
  • — Links to your Instagram, Strava club, or website
  • — A contact who can speak for the organization
  • — A couple sentences on why your crew exists

Questions before applying? Email membership@rundivision1.com.
