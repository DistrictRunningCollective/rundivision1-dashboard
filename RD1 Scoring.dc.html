How Season Zero works

Different cities. Same division.

Every RD1 crew creates an official 5K circuit in its city. Runners register with RD1 and complete their local circuit. Three cups score three different things — speed, turnout, and impact — and each crew's rank in each cup earns standardized points that add up to the overall standings.

01 / Performance Championship

Fastest verified times, one board

A crew's Championship score is its fastest 3 verified times this circuit, wherever those runners placed. Rank on the combined board sets the points.

02 / Club Cup

Most runners under standard

Each crew sets and publishes its own approved circuit standard — a finish time it considers a completed run. The crew with the most runners beating its own standard leads the cup.
