Four ways in.

Network membership, the live season, the story behind it, and the teams carrying it all.

01 / The Network

Keep your identity. Gain infrastructure.

RunDivision is a member association for independently owned, community-led run crews. Affiliation brings shared infrastructure, paid partnerships, member grants, and a voice in shaping the network, without asking crews to give up what they built.

Explore the Network →
02 / Season Zero

Ten cities. One division.

The inaugural season connects the crews through a circuit of locally curated city challenges. Each host crew picks its own route, and every field feeds three national cups: Championship, Club, and Impact.

Enter Season Zero → View standings →
03 / Studios

The people, places and crews create the league.

RunDivision Studios is the media arm documenting the crews, athletes and cities behind the movement. Route stories, City Day recaps, and profiles of the founding crews, published year-round.

See the stories →
04 / Teams

Ten teams, ten cities.

Every RD1 team is an independent crew competing as its city's representative. See who is racing, who is organizing, and how each team is scoring across the three season cups.
